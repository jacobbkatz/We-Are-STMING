# STM v2 at UCSD: budget

`STM_v2_budget.xlsx` is the budget for building a second STM at UCSD. It is written for Prof. Oscar Vasquez and the UCSD budget office. It has two tabs:

- **v2 budget.** This is the main tab. Every part is grouped by what it does in the microscope. Each part has a plain description, a note on whether it is new in v2 and why, the vendor, the part number, the quantity, the price and a link.
  - Sections 1 to 7 are a complete, working microscope built around v1's piezo disc, which the main board drives directly. Section 7 is the batteries: the whole microscope runs on batteries, with no wall power.
  - Section 8 is the optional **piezo tube upgrade** (Prof. Vasquez's suggestion): the tube, its mount and wiring, a battery-powered high-voltage driver, and its own shipping and import fees. One **Yes/No switch** adds it to or removes it from the total. A spare tube and a machined metal head plate are optional rows inside it, set to **No**.
  - Section 9 lists items a UCSD lab may already have. Choosing **Yes** in its last column takes that item off the total. The LCR meter only counts when the upgrade is on.
- **v1 (what it cost).** This tab shows what the first instrument actually cost, $1,375.93. The 42 Amazon purchases are grouped into plain categories.

| v2 total | UCSD supplies nothing in section 9 | UCSD supplies everything in section 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Without the piezo upgrade | $2,327.27 | $1,317.13 |
| With the piezo upgrade (spare tube and metal plate not included) | $3,000.38 | $1,942.83 |

v1 actually cost $1,375.93.

Prices were checked on 2026-10-21, in USD. The JLCPCB circuit boards are priced at what v1 paid in July 2026. The high-voltage driver circuit is not designed yet, so its parts are estimates, as are the tube mount and wiring parts. Every total includes shipping, the 35% US tariff on circuit boards, other import fees, 7.75% sales tax and 10% spare money.
