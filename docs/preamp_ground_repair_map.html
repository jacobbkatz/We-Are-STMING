Repair

  1. Flip to the underside. Scrape dot 1.

    Scalpel tip, light strokes, until a shiny copper ring about 1 mm across shows. Only the green coating comes off, so don’t dig.

  2. Two checks on dot 1, before any wire

    a. One probe on dot 1 (underside), the other on spot 1 on top, the chip’s left side, third leg down. MUST BEEP: proves it’s pin 3’s dot.

    b. One probe on dot 1, the other on hole 1. MUST BE SILENT: proves pin 3 really is disconnected, with no tiny chip leg to miss.

    If a is silent or b beeps, stop and tell Claude before doing anything else.

    a. Dot 1 to pin 3
    b. Dot 1 to hole 1

  3. Scrape dot 2, and check it

    Same way. a. Dot 2 to spot 2 on top (right-hand pad of the tiny part C4): MUST BEEP. b. Dot 2 to hole 1: MUST BE SILENT.

    a. Dot 2 to C4 pad
    b. Dot 2 to hole 1

  4. Wires 1 and 2: done

    Dots 1 and 2 are each wired to hole 1’s joint.

  5. Undo any top-side attempt

    If wire 3 or 4 is partly soldered on top, remove it. Check under the magnifier that no loose wire or solder is left near R1 or hole 2.

  6. Dot 3 (C2’s): scrape and check

    Underside, near the bottom edge, roughly level with hole 5. Nothing else is close to it. Scrape it like dots 1 and 2.

    a. Dot 3 to the right-hand end of C2 on top: MUST BEEP. b. Dot 3 to hole 1: MUST BE SILENT.

    a. Dot 3 to C2 end
    b. Dot 3 to hole 1

  7. Dot 4 (C3’s): check first, then scrape half

    Before scraping: on top, C3’s right-hand pad to hole 2: MUST BE SILENT. If it beeps, stop: C3 is already touching +15 V and must not be grounded.

    This dot’s tiny copper tail points left at hole 2’s pad and ends less than a tenth of a millimetre from it (amber ring). Scrape only the right-hand half of the dot, the side away from the holes (red half-ring). Leave the tail covered.

    After scraping: a. dot 4 to C3’s right-hand pad: MUST BEEP. b. dot 4 to hole 1: MUST BE SILENT. c. dot 4 to hole 2: MUST BE SILENT.

    Before: C3 pad to hole 2
    a. Dot 4 to C3 pad
    b. Dot 4 to hole 1
    c. Dot 4 to hole 2

  8. Wires 3 and 4 (underside)

    Wire 3 (orange): dot 3 to dot 2’s joint, about 5 mm. Dot 2 is already ground through wire 2.

    Wire 4 (blue): from the right-hand side of dot 4, straight up about 2 mm, then over to hole 1’s joint. Keep its solder on dot 4’s right-hand half, and the wire clear of hole 2’s pad.

    Tin each wire end and each dot first, then 1 second per joint. Re-touching dot 2 and hole 1 can loosen wires 1 and 2, so the final checks test all four.

  9. Check the repair

    Top side, meter on beep, black probe on hole 1. Tap each result.

    Spot 1: chip pin 3 should beep now
    Spot 2: C4 right-hand pad should beep
    Spot 3: C2 right-hand end should beep
    Spot 4: C3 right-hand pad should beep
    IN: chip pin 2 (left side, second leg) MUST stay silent. A beep means the input is shorted to ground
    Hole 2 short chirp only, never a continuous beep
    Hole 5 short chirp only, never a continuous beep
