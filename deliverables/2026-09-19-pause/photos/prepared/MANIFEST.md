# Prepared photographs — manifest

**Fifteen images, cropped, annotated and captioned for the manual, the report and the poster.**

Every one is regenerated by one command, from the repository root:

```bash
python3 deliverables/2026-09-19-pause/photos/prepare_photos.py
```

The script reads the full-resolution decoded originals from the lead's scratchpad when they are
still there, and falls back to the 1600 px working copies in `Images/ours/` when they are not. The
fallback is softer and otherwise identical. **The full-resolution copies are not in the repository
and must not be committed.**

---

## The rules these were made under

- **Crop, rotate and annotate only.** No factual content is changed: nothing is retouched,
  composited, colour-shifted or reconstructed.
- **Every arrow and label names something visible in the frame it sits on.** A label that is a
  reading rather than something Jacob or Nuh stated carries **`(READ)`**.
- **Every caption carries its source path, its IMG_ number and its capture time**, so the Drive
  original can always be found again. Capture times are camera-local; the EXIF carries no offset
  tag, so their relation to the UTC session logs is an inference.
- **No scale bar appears on any image**, because no photograph here has a known scale.
- **No number has been measured off any of these** — in particular not `d`, the tip-to-pivot
  distance, which `Images/ours/README.md` records as unmeasurable from photographs after three
  attempts that differed by more than a factor of two.

## Notes for whoever lays these out

- **Each image already carries its caption in a black bar below the picture.** If your layout has
  its own caption style, crop the bar off and take the text from the table above plus the frame's
  row in `../IMAGE_INVENTORY.md` — do not paraphrase away the `READ`/`SAID` marks or the UNKNOWNs.
- **Two images carry faces: 13 and 14.** Jacob confirmed on 2026-09-19 that a team photograph may
  appear on the poster. **That permission covers the poster.** Anything beyond that is his call,
  not ours — and several more frames with faces are listed in `../IMAGE_INVENTORY.md` §B2 if a
  different one is wanted.
- **06 and 08 are the two frames that add a part number the repository did not have.** If either
  ends up in the report, the claim it supports is only "this is the part in the photograph", not
  any specification of it.
