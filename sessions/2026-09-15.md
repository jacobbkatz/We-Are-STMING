## 4. New faults

**None in the hardware.** Nothing broke and nothing turned out to be broken.

**Three in our own tooling**, all in `Code/pc/check_facts.py`, all recorded in `STATUS.md` under
"Known code issues" and **none of them fixed:**

1. **A RETIRED row is silently dead if its replacement's first number also appears in the stale
   line.** Lines 394–396 skip any line containing the replacement's first `[\d.]+` match, assuming
   such a line is a correction table. The natural wording of this session's retirement was
   `0.076 mm interference` → `0.076 mm CLEARANCE`, where only the word changes. Both carry `0.076`,
   so **the row fired on zero of two deliberate re-introductions.** Worked around by rewording the
   replacement as "CLEARANCE, not interference", which has no shared number; both rows then fired.
   **The same weakness leaves the existing `±5 V` → `±3 V` row nearly dead**, because its
   replacement's first token is `3` and almost every line contains a 3.
2. **`~~` excuses a whole line.** In a `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` table row where only the *question*
   is struck through and the *answer* cell is live and wrong, nothing is flagged. **That is exactly
   how two stale press-fit rows survived.**
3. **The failure hint tells you to add the word `was`**, which is not in the `EXCUSES` regex, so
   following the hint does not clear the warning.

## 6. False trails and decisions not taken

- **Part C was skipped tonight, by Jacob's decision**, on the grounds that our supplies cannot
  resolve the 1 mA criterion. **He was right about the criterion**, which is now corrected, and the
  test is **resequenced rather than dropped**: it now runs after the wash and before the input node
  is built. That is better than either end of the argument — the board is clean by then, and a
  fault is still found while the board is bare and reworkable. **Rework after the node is built
  means flux and a second wash at the one spot that cannot be cleaned afterwards.**
- **The datasheet route to the OPA627's quiescent current was blocked all evening.** ti.com,
  mouser.com, alldatasheet.com and a Carnegie Science mirror are all refused by this session's
  network. **No absolute expected current was given to Jacob**, deliberately, rather than quoting a
  remembered figure — `CLAUDE.md` is explicit that a remembered or search-summary number is not a
  datasheet. **Nothing was blocked by it:** Part C's pass criteria are a limit check and a symmetry
  check, neither of which needs the absolute number.
- **A "nice diagram" was asked for and built**, but the more useful output was the question behind
  it: Jacob asked whether Part B was something he had already done. **It is a different test at a
  different place**, and the fact that the two look alike is worth remembering — the 2026-09-14
  readings probed the board, Part B probes the leads.

## 9. Process lessons

- **"Fixed" needs a grep, and the grep needs to happen before the word is used.** Commit `04b15a4`
  corrected the standoff claim in four files and its own message said it was done. **It was live in
  six more**, two of them actionable at the bench that same night. `CLAUDE.md` §7 rule 1 already
  says a defect is a class rather than an instance — **this is the second time that rule has caught
  something after the fix was announced**, which suggests the rule is right and the habit is not yet
  automatic.
- **A checker that cannot fail is not a checker.** The new RETIRED row looked correct and was dead.
  It was only found because §7 rule 2 was followed literally: the fault was re-introduced in a
  scratch file to watch the check go red, and it stayed green. **The rule earned its place tonight.**
- **The user's objection was better than the specification.** "Within 1 mA" was written by me and
  defended by nothing. Jacob pushed back from the bench, and the reason he was right — C2 is being
  replaced anyway, so the sensitivity was never load-bearing — **was available in our own safety
  rule 14 the whole time.**
- **Two readings were exposed before acting on them and one was wrong.** The water-rinse question
  produced a genuine change of plan, and the missing oven that gated it had never been recorded
  either way. **The question cost one sentence.**
