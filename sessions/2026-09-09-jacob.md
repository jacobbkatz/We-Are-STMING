## 6. False trails, and things ruled out

### 6.1 C2 polarity is not the 119 nA. Ruled out by argument, not by measurement.

`N$4` has **exactly one connection in the entire design** — `IC1.-IN`. One pad, no track, no via.
C2 connects the −15 V net to GND and touches neither.

**And C2's two pads sit at −15 V and 0 V whichever way the part inside is facing.** The copper,
the soldermask and any contamination on them see an identical voltage map either way, so the
surface-leakage path toward the input pad is unchanged. The only thing polarity alters is leakage
*inside* the capacitor, anode to cathode, which flows −15 V to GND entirely within the supply loop.

**Nor the other symptoms:**

| Symptom | Why C2 does not explain it |
|---|---|
| 45-minute warm-up drift | C2 sits **7.1 mm** from IC1. A 6032 part on a 20 × 15 mm board settles thermally in a minute or two, not 45. Raising it 1 °C needs roughly 10 mW — about **0.7 mA** of leakage, obvious on the bench supply |
| Noise | OPA627 PSRR is over 100 dB, and the recorded noise is probably the floating `PREAMP−` |
| Anything at all | The −15 V rail measured **−15.237 V** on 2026-09-07. A significantly leaking C2 would load it |

**Do not re-rank the 119 nA candidates because of C2.** The superglue at the PTFE standoff is
unaffected and remains the lead.

### 6.2 Continuity testing cannot detect capacitor polarity

Jacob recalled testing continuity at C2. **A capacitor is a DC open in both directions**, and
buzzing its terminals identifies which *net* each pad is on — which is the same either way. Only
reading the stripe under magnification settles it. The earlier test was not wrong, it was the
wrong instrument for this question.

### 6.3 An inference from document structure, presented as a finding

`docs/BOM.md` §3 lists preamp parts individually and `gerbers/README.md` prices only *controller*
assembly, so this session concluded the preamp board was hand-populated. **It was JLCPCB PCBA.**
See §8.

## 8. Process lessons — why this session made repeated context errors

**Jacob, plainly:** *"you've gotten a lot dumber and less intelligent since our last session."*
Worth recording honestly, because the diagnosis is actionable.

**Checked, not guessed:** the model did not change. `configured_model` and `last_served_model` both
`claude-opus-5`, effort high, no fallback.

**What did change: this session was created 2026-09-01 and had been compacted** — it ran out of
context and was replaced by a summary. The raw conversation was gone.

**The four errors all have the same shape.** Each was a *physical* fact — what we own, how a board
was assembled, what a supplier did — inferred from a *design* document and stated before being
flagged as an inference:

| Inferred | From | Actually |
|---|---|---|
| Preamp was hand-populated | `docs/BOM.md` layout | **JLCPCB PCBA, 2 off** |
| We own the 11301 | `docs/BOM.md` "CONFIRMED" + shopping list | **We own the 11311** |
| Nobody had checked C1/C2 polarity | nothing said otherwise | **JLCPCB raised it at ordering** |
| (before compaction) A v2 box boss position was safe | the mesh | **It landed on the input node.** Caught by Nuh |

**The root cause is not the model and not the compaction. It is that nothing in this repository
recorded what we physically own.** All seventeen documents were checked. `docs/BOM.md` records what
was *specified*; a netlist records what was *drawn*; a shopping list records what someone *intended
to buy*. **None of them is an inventory**, and reading one as if it were produced every error above.

Those facts lived in Jacob's head and in a Gmail thread. When the context was compacted they went
with it, and the gaps got filled by inference.

> **`CLAUDE.md` §3b says search before declaring something unknown. §3c says get the number before
> ordering a test. §3d, added today, covers the case neither reaches: the answer is in nobody's
> file. Ask, or write UNKNOWN. An order confirmation outranks every document in this repository.**

**A second, smaller lesson.** Purchase advice was given, reversed one message later, and reversed
on the standoff too. **Reason a purchase decision all the way through before putting it in front of
someone who is about to spend money.** A correction costs them a second order and shipping.
