## 3. The cleaning-kit question, and where the answer already was

**Jacob asked whether distilled water and lint-free wipes were needed, given he already has IPA and
brushes.**

**Most of the answer was already written down** — `docs/PREAMP_SHOPPING_LIST.md` has a line-by-line
justification for all five cleaning items, written 2026-09-08 and revised 2026-09-09. **Credit to
whoever wrote that table**; it is the reason this took minutes rather than a re-derivation.

**What this session added to it, and what it took away:**

| Item | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Lint-free wipes | Justified on fibre-shedding grounds only | **Upgraded.** The stronger reason is that dissolution is not removal: the wipe is the step that carries the rosin off the board. Brushes lift, IPA dissolves, **wipes and swabs remove** |
| Foam/polyester swabs | On the list | **Flagged as separate from wipes and brushes** — they are what reaches around the standoff, which is the input node itself |
| Distilled water | "Buy. IPA dissolves rosin but not ionic salts" | **Downgraded to optional, and possibly harmful.** RMA791 is ROL0 — halide-free. `sessions/2026-09-09-jacob.md` §11.4 had already drawn this line from the other side, calling the water rinse the risk-carrying step **only for the halide-bearing RA alternative** |

**Confidence, stated plainly.** The ROL0 classification is the repository's own earlier research, not
re-confirmed today. **chipquik.com is blocked by the network egress proxy from this session, exactly
as recorded on 2026-09-09** — two separate fetch attempts, both `EGRESS_BLOCKED`. The white-bloom
mechanism is from trade sources, not a manufacturer document. **This is a downgrade, not a settled
prohibition.**

## 5. What was ruled out, and a new defect in the checker

**`check_facts.py`'s link check was tested by deliberately breaking a link, per `CLAUDE.md` §7 rule
2 — a test that has never failed has not been run. It passed while the link was broken.**

**The defect.** `check_links()` matches only backtick-wrapped repo-root-relative paths
(`` `docs/FACTS.md` ``). **It never looks at markdown link targets** — the `[...](...)` form, where the path sits in the
parentheses rather than in backticks.
A link whose visible text is right and whose target rots sails straight through, which is precisely
the failure its own docstring says it exists to prevent.

**Nothing is broken today.** All markdown link targets across the 38 live `.md` files were checked
by hand this session and **every one resolves**, including the new ones. **The hole is in the guard,
not in the repository.** Recorded rather than fixed — fixing it is its own piece of work and should
be done with `CLAUDE.md` §7 rule 1 in mind: a defect is a class, so the fix has to be tested against
a deliberately broken link before it is believed.
