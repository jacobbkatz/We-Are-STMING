## 5. What was got wrong along the way

1. **A distance nobody has recorded.** Rewriting the About lede, Claude wrote that the two of them
   were *"four hundred miles apart for most of the build"*. **Nothing in this repository records
   any distance.** Caught and removed the same minute, but it is exactly the §3d failure: filling a
   gap about physical reality by inference. The lede was deleted rather than replaced.

2. **Sizing the nav from a synthetic element.** To decide how many links fit, Claude measured a
   hidden `div` built off-DOM with the same declared font. It said **793 px**. The real rendered
   nodes measure **873 px** — 10% out, enough to be wrong about the answer. **Measure the real
   node.** The first fix shipped on that bad number and the bar wrapped to two rows.

3. **Fixing a breakpoint instead of a class.** The SVG labels were fixed at ≤760 px and the checker
   then found **40 labels at 9.1 px at 768 px** — the tablet band, which the media query did not
   reach. The real fix was to make the drawing's `min-width` equal its viewBox width at every
   width, and, for the hero chart which has to fit rather than scroll, a ladder computed so each
   band's narrow end still clears 12 px.

4. **A message sent to the wrong worker.** Figure findings went to the agent doing the documents,
   which **correctly refused, stayed inside its scope, and said so** rather than quietly widening
   its job. The findings were forwarded. It also found **a second plotted British spelling that the
   original message had missed** — `characterised` in fig09's subtitle, which renders into the
   image.

## 6. What was deliberately NOT done

- **`docs/`, `STATUS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` keep their British spelling.** The brief was the website.
  `check_facts.py` matches RETIRED qualifiers against real wording, so a blanket spelling change
  across the register and the protocol needs its own pass with that checker watched, not a sweep at
  the end of a long session. **The repository is therefore mixed: the deliverables and the site are
  US English, the operating documents are not.**
- **`sessions/` untouched.** History keeps its original numbers and its original spelling.
- **The `parts.json` base64 wrapper** (1.9 MB, 25% of which is the base64 padding) was left alone:
  GitHub Pages gzips, and the decoded payload compresses better than the base64 does, so the saving
  is largely absorbed. Worth revisiting only if the host stops compressing.

## 7b. A correction from Jacob, mid-session

**`SAID` Jacob, 2026-09-21: *"We used etched tips the whole time."*** Asked to choose between
readings, he confirmed the tips were **sharp when fitted and blunted in use**.

**This answers two things `docs/INVENTORY.md` has carried as UNKNOWN since 2026-09-19** — item (4),
whether the etching setup was ever used successfully, and item (5), whether any fitted tip came off
it. Both yes. **`README.md`'s *"a cut tungsten tip, 0.25 mm wire cut at 45 degrees"* was simply
wrong** and is corrected; no tip in this project was ever cut.

**What it changes.** Tip bluntness moves from *an independent blocker* to *a consequence of the
snap*: the instrument drives the tip into the sample, and the last one fitted was pressed into the
gold four separate times in one morning, twice for over half an hour. **The fix is at the approach,
not at the tip** — a fresh tip only stays sharp if it stops being driven in. The website's blocker 3
was rewritten to say so, and it now points at blocker 1 rather than standing alone.

**What is still unknown, and still needs one sentence each:** the NaOH concentration, the voltage,
and the counter-electrode material. **Nobody can repeat the etch from this repository.**

**What is NOT reconciled, and was left standing rather than edited away.** The bench logs record
Jacob's own words on two tips: *"horribly probably extremly blunt"* (2026-09-16) and *"new tip its
gonna be very blunt but that what we have for today"* (2026-09-19, ~03:00) — **the second said
before that tip was used that day**, which does not sit easily with "sharp when fitted". Session
logs are history and keep their wording. **One sentence from Jacob would settle it** and it is
flagged in `docs/INVENTORY.md` rather than resolved by inference.

**Process note.** This is the §3d case working as intended: no amount of searching would have found
it, because it was never written down. It took one question, and the answer changed a live page, a
blocker, two documents and the inventory.
