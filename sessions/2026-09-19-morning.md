## 4. What the day establishes, and what it does not

**Established, measured:**
1. **Z controls the current, but the junction is not a clean tunnelling gap at any bias tried** (§3.10-3.11).
2. **The contact is hysteretic and snaps**, reproducibly.
3. **The tip-sample gap moves by more than the whole Z range over seconds to minutes with nothing moving,
   in both directions** (§3.8, 3.9, 3.12).
4. **Hand-sets always end out of reach after release; the 20-step motor approach can find the gold; the
   motor cannot release a hard contact** (§3.2-3.8, 3.12).
5. **A big upward Z jump can put a false contact on the next read** (§3.5).

**NOT established** — each is a live hypothesis for the break:
- **What moves the gold.** Candidates: the leaf on its paper (compliant, lifts and follows the tip); the
  plate on its rubber bands and ball contacts (viscoelastic creep after every push); thermal motion of the
  printed head. **Plate on all three balls (`SAID`), and the bag was off.**
- **The Z scale.** A decade per ~1,800 counts would be tunnelling only if Z moved the tip ~100x less than
  assumed. The snaps and hysteresis argue for a soft contact instead, but the scale is still unmeasured.
- **Thread play in the side screws.** Proposed at 11:5x to explain the hand-set losses. **The touch test
  (§3.6) found no effect of a still hand**, so it is not supported; the losses are equally explained by the
  gold's own swing.

## 6. False trails

- **"No hand back-off: turn in until the beeps, and let the motor retract."** The hand over-travels by
  micrometres and the motor cannot undo it (§3.3).
- **"Thread play in the printed side-screw threads makes the gold spring back on release."** Not supported
  by the touch test (§3.6); not needed to explain anything the swing does not.
- **"Synchronise the scanner to the gold's motion frequency"** (Jacob's idea). A good idea for a periodic
  disturbance; **the motion is aperiodic** (§5), so there is nothing to lock to.
- **"The one-sweep IN blips during nudges are the gold resting in range."** They vanish the instant the hand
  lets go (§3.6).

## 8. Process lessons

- **Asking a question is not a place to stop.** After Jacob answered, the session sat idle for ~30
  minutes. A question must come with the next action already queued.
- **Check the clock before writing a time.** Two estimates today were off by 6-7 minutes.
- **A red check that cannot go red has not tested anything** — twice today (ztest, touch_and_hold) the
  first version of the check could not fail. `CLAUDE.md` §7 rule 2, again.
- **Measure what the controller needs before tuning it.** The loop constant had been carried from the
  2026-09-17 junction to a different tip and sample; one Z test showed it 18x off.
- **When the answer is "the mechanics", say so and stop scanning.** Three scan attempts would not have
  changed §3.12.
- **Write data rows as they are taken.** Three CSVs (and the swing log's) were lost today because they were
  written only on a clean exit and the processes were stopped.
- **A second verification workflow**, checking every number in this log against the raw files, **was still
  running when this was committed.** Any corrections it finds follow in a later commit, marked as such.
