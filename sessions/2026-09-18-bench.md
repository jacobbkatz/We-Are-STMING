### 3.2 First power-up and the first approach

**Approach 1**, `--z-retracted low --motor-toward-sample negative --motor-step 5 --max-steps 600

### 3.3 The hand-set: about 2.2 mm

Parked Z and bias at midscale; USB out, supplies off, **preamp DB9 unplugged**; meter on beep from
the tip holder to the orange bias wire; the two side-by-side screws turned in equal small amounts.

**`SAID`:** *"i had to turn the screws a ton more in before i got anythign 7 ish turns"* — **about

**So the rebuild left the tip about 2 mm further from the sample than before.** At 3.9 nm per step

### 3.4 Contact, confirmed by the bias flip

Re-power (uptime 25.7 s). **Touch check at Z 10000:** 0 V mean **+142**, −0.5 V mean **−44** — clear.
LED1-LED4 dark (`SAID`).

**Approach 2**, same flags, `--max-steps 3500`: noise 1,821, threshold 10,923. **Contact after 5
motor steps at Z 25,800, reading 20,878 counts (about 65 nA).**

**The bias flip at the onset, 01:35:06** (`flip1.log`), Z crept up in 50-count steps from 10000 to
the first reading over 3,000, at Z 11,900:

| Sample | Readings |
|---|---|
| −0.5 V | 6,126 to 11,663 |
| +0.5 V | **−8,430 to −11,170** |
| 0 V | −1,264 to 1,987 |
| −0.5 V again | 12,647 to 15,511 |

**The reading follows the bias sign and is about the same size both ways. A real junction.** The
sign matches 2026-09-16's dummy junction: negative sample voltage reads positive.

**Then it would not stay out.** Already before the flip, the "baseline" at Z 10000 read 3,362-7,832.
Within a minute the reading **pinned at +32,767 at Z 10000, and stayed pinned at Z 0**, the fully
retracted end. At +0.5 V it pinned at −32,768; at 0 V it read 97. **So a real contact that Z could
not pull out of.** **`MTMV +500` with Z at 0 cleared it**, and 60 s at Z 0 read flat near zero.

> **Deviation from the rules, recorded.** `CLAUDE.md` says to park Z at midscale before the motor
> moves. **The motor was moved with Z at 0 instead, because midscale extends the tip toward the
> sample and would have pressed it further in.** The rule's purpose — never have the tip extended
> when the motor moves — is better served by the fully retracted end.

### 3.6 Approach 3, and a contact that would not clear

**Where a single step changed things, it moved the onset 14,000-29,000 counts.** In between, the

**Slope at the onset, every pass:** from about 1,000-8,500 counts to over 10,000 in **25-75 Z

### 3.8 The fast tracker, and two claims Claude made and withdrew

`fasttrack.py` samples the gap at about 95 per second by short climbs to 1,000 counts. **Its
results, all provisional — see the caveat below:**

- **Hold A, just after positioning:** onset sd 5,375. **Hold B, about 80 s later:** sd **1,578**.
- **X:** about −0.25 onset counts per X count, the 2026-09-17 sign. **Y:** not resolved, under ±1,000.
- **Five minutes** (`ft_long1.log`): sd 1,000-6,500 in 10 s chunks, **no settling trend**, the
  median steady near 19,200 for a minute then falling to about 4,800 over the next. The strongest
  line of the last chunk's spectrum was near 6 Hz, in 2026-09-17's 5-20 Hz band.
- **Single steps** (`lash_run1.log`, stopped by hand): **20 approach steps with the median unmoved
  at 15,000-17,000**, and long runs in both directions with no systematic change.

> **Claim withdrawn 1: "the wobble settles after the motor stops."** Said to Jacob on the strength of
> holds A and B. **The five-minute run did not reproduce it.** Nothing was written down before it
> was withdrawn.
>
> **Claim withdrawn 2: "the fast tracker's onsets are an artefact."** At 02:33 it found onsets **with
> the bias at 0 V**, which a tunnelling current cannot do. **Tested before anything was written:** a
> Z step of +800, −4000 and +4000 at 0 V gives at most **611 counts** immediately after, below the
> 1,000 threshold. **And a slow static scan found the reason: a hard contact at Z 42,000-43,000
> that reads full scale at 0 V too** — the DAC-offset effect 2026-09-17 §3.11 already recorded.
> **So the tracker was mostly finding real snap-ins, not artefacts.** Its numbers locate snap-in
> events, not a tunnelling onset, which is why they stay provisional.

- **The tip snaps into contact** rather than approaching through a tunnelling range. §3.7. A soft,
  bent placeholder tip jumping to the surface is the simplest explanation; untested.

  0, so the tip was left in contact (reading 7,233) for about 30 s until `backoff.py` cleared it.
  A script that moves the tip in should refuse a zero retract.

## 9. Process lessons

- **A result that would void earlier data needs its discriminating test BEFORE it is announced.**
  Twice tonight a conclusion went to Jacob and was withdrawn minutes later. The 0 V test, and the
  transient test that settled it, each took under a minute.
- **Precedence of what is in the room over what is in a photograph held again.** The off-centre tip
  from `2026-09-18.md` §3.5 was a photographic reading; Jacob's statement replaced it in one line.
- **A calibration taken during a reversal measures the slack, not the step.** Worth checking on any
  lead-screw instrument before a number is carried into `docs/FACTS.md` or `docs/INVENTORY.md`.
- **Simulated-junction tests before hardware paid for themselves again** (§7).
