## 3. Measurements

### 3.1 Before power: bounce and paper slip (`SAID`)

**Bounce:** *"probably about three but the dampaning stops the bounces after like three"* — read as
about three bounces a second, dying out in about three. **Up and down, not sideways**, which Claude
specified because a sideways push excites the ~1 Hz pendulum swing and would have read as "open".
**Three bounces to about 10% is a damping ratio near 0.12**, which caps the resonant gain near 4.

**Paper slip:** not done. *"we are sturggling the get the paper in with out shaking the machine there
is 100% a gap and this test isnt needed lets move on"*.

back and latches. Tested on a simulated junction first, including a deliberate failure. Jacob turned

- **01:19:49: contact, 4082 counts (12.7 nA)**, from 6 counts the reading before. Z pulled back at

## 4. New faults

- **CLAUDE'S ERROR — two approaches ran at 0 V bias.** `onset0.py` and `clearcheck.py` each ended
  by zeroing the bias; `stm_approach.py` never set one. **At 0 V no tunnelling current flows, so
  the threshold could only trip on metal contact** — the DAC's few-mV offset across a near-short.
  **The new tip was driven into the gold at least twice.** **Fixed in the tool** (§7): it sets the
  bias itself and refuses to approach within 0.05 V of zero.
- **`stm_approach.py`'s direction-finding mode reported a direction from no evidence** when the hit
  was midscale itself. **Fixed** (§7).
- **The laptop beeper's alarm was not heard.** The detection and the retract worked.
- **Claude left the tip in contact**: `zdir.py`, which had no watchdog, ended with Z parked at 50000
  while 27000-32000 counts flowed; +200 motor steps cleared it a minute later. **And several of
  tonight's scripts parked Z at 0 as "retracted"**, an assumption this tip's data does not support
  (§3.9).
- **The gold wanders by more than the Z range** (§3.8) — the blocker.

## 9. Process lessons

- **A script that zeroes the bias on exit hands the next tool a zero-bias instrument.** The fix
  belongs in the tool that needs the bias, not in the discipline of whoever ran something before it.
- **"Retract" is only a direction once the direction is known.** Several of tonight's scripts sent
  Z to 0 as a safe position under an assumption that tonight's data does not support.
- **Work out what a test can see before running it.** The clear-tip stamp test was flagged before it
  ran and its result was reported for what it is.
