## 6. False trails

### The print material, and it was a false trail of my own making as much as anyone's

**Mid-session Jacob said: *"we didnt use petg-cf remeber look up what we really used"*.** That is a
`SAID` about physical reality from one of the two people who built it, which `CLAUDE.md` §3d ranks
above every document here, so it was taken seriously and the design files were opened.

**What the slicer files say.** All five `CAD/prints/print-plates/*.3mf`, saved 2026-07-03, select
**Bambu PA-CF** (`filament_id` GFN03), nozzle 290 °C, bed 100 °C. **Every object on every plate is
assigned to extruder 1**, which is that filament — `PiezoPlate` and `SamplePlate` included, both of
which sit in the tip-sample loop. **PLA is loaded in slot 2 and no object uses it**, which at least
rules out the material `CAD/prints/README.md` warns about in four places.

**Then Jacob withdrew it: *"sorry it is petg-cf"*.** So the repository was right, the 2026-09-07
entry stands, and **nothing needed correcting in any of the eight live places that carry it** —
`docs/BOM.md` §10, `docs/FACTS.md`, `README.md`, `CAD/prints/README.md`, `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`,
`docs/ENGINEERING_REFERENCE.md` §11, `docs/START_HERE_gotchas.md` and `STATUS.md`.

**Three things worth keeping from it anyway:**

1. **`CAD/prints/README.md` had already found the extruder assignment** and says so at line 271,
   under a banner explaining the discrepancy. **The search re-derived what was already written
   down** — the §3b failure mode, in miniature, and it cost a few minutes rather than a day only
   because the file was read in the same pass.
2. **A row now sits in `docs/INVENTORY.md` recording the re-confirmation**, so the question is not
   researched a third time. Two confirmations from Jacob, eleven days apart.
3. **A mechanism was investigated and then correctly dropped.** If the parts *had* been nylon, its
   moisture uptake would have been a real drift candidate — nylon swells as it takes up water from
   the air, typically by more than it moves with temperature. **PETG-CF takes up far less**, which
   `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` had already noted on 2026-09-07. **It is not a candidate and was not
   written into the drift list.**

### What was NOT concluded, deliberately

**No coefficient of thermal expansion has been written into this repository.** The manufacturer's
datasheet could not be fetched — **this container's network policy blocks `bambulab.com`,
`wiki.bambulab.com` and the distributor mirrors** — and a search-result summary is not a datasheet.
**On the Keystone standoff this project took three web-sourced answers on one part number and all
three were wrong** (`CLAUDE.md` §7). The thermal arithmetic in §3 is therefore given as a bracket
with both unknowns named, and it is labelled as such in the plan, in `STATUS.md` and in the draft
reply.

### Textbook piezo creep does not fit the fast excursion, and our own data is what says so

Zahl's *"look up: Piezo creep"* is well aimed, but **creep decays in one direction**, and the 6.4 s
excursion **reverses and overshoots its own starting point**
(`deliverables/2026-09-19-pause/LEAD_VERIFICATION.md` V6). **That is a discriminator he does not
have**, and it is in the draft reply. **It does not clear the piezo for the slower motions**, and it
does not touch his second point — joints and stressed points creeping for a long time — which lands
squarely on candidates 2 and 7.

## 9. Process lessons

**Jacob's mid-session correction was wrong, and taking it seriously anyway was still right.** He
said the parts were not PETG-CF; they are. But `CLAUDE.md` §3d exists because inferring physical
facts from design files went wrong four times in one day, and **the cost of checking was a few
minutes while the cost of ignoring a builder's correction is a wrong fact inherited by the other
computer.** The useful discipline was not doubting him — it was **not writing anything down until
it was settled.** Eight files would have been edited and then edited back.

**A defect is a class, and this time the class had a second half.** `CLAUDE.md` §7 rule 1 says grep
for every occurrence before reporting a fix. That was done — two live occurrences of *"at least
1.94 nm"*. **But the grep only finds the phrase, not the argument built on it.** Flipping "at
least" to "under" would have passed every check in this repository and left a paragraph whose
conclusion no longer followed from its premise. **Re-read what the sentence was for, not just
whether the number is right.**
